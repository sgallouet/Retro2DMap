Polished illustrated JRPG ground artwork.

Each square must be a seamlessly repeating texture, left matching right and top matching bottom. Carry features naturally across opposite borders. Uniform lighting and coverage, no central focal point, no vignette, no edge shadow, no frame. Entirely opaque. No props, plants as objects, flowers, trees, characters, cast shadows, labels, grid or scenery. Repaint these input MATERIAL TILES, not a map. Output just the 2-tile sheet.
